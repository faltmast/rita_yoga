# Rita Artiushina - Brand Guide

## Overview
Yoga teacher based in Berlin. Website serves as a professional calling card to get noticed by studios, land teaching jobs, and accept private bookings.

---

## Target Audience

- **Age:** 20-45 years
- **Location:** Berlin (expanding later)
- **Experience:** Beginner yogis
- **Demographics:** All genders - busy professionals, students, wellness enthusiasts
- **Motivation:** Stress relief, fitness, spirituality, detaching from work
- **Mindset:** People who care about their health in general

---

## Teaching Style

- **Traditions:** Hatha alignment, Yin yoga
- **Pace:** Starts slow, builds up
- **Elements:** Breathwork, meditation, mudras, yoga philosophy
- **Duration:** 60-75 minute classes
- **Philosophy:** Inner work, working with limitations, removal of competition

---

## Differentiator

- Corporate burnout survivor
- Yoga helped her survive dark moments, changed her mindset, new perspective
- Transforms moods, lifts people up
- Brings real energy into the practice
- **Credentials:** 200+ hours teacher training from Yoga Circle Academy Berlin
- **Experience:** 5 years practice (2 years intense)

---

## Offerings

- **Primary:** Studio classes (seeking positions)
- **Secondary:** Private 1:1 sessions (negotiated directly)
- **Future:** Corporate yoga, retreats, festival bookings
- **Not offered:** Online classes
